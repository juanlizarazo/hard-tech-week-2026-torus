The cleanest hour depends on how you count

Average CO2 divides all fossil emissions by all generation. Marginal CO2 is what the next MWh adds. They rank the same 24 hours very differently.

Each line is one hour of the day. Rank 1 is the cleanest hour, rank 24 the dirtiest.

  • Left column: the hour's rank by average CO2. Right column: its rank by marginal CO2.
  • Brown lines: the three hours whose rank changes most. Grey lines: every other hour.
  • A steep line means the two measures disagree about that hour.

Why it matters: a battery that charges in the "cleanest" average hour can still be adding CO2 at the margin.
