Every circle is a power plant that reports to the PACE balancing area, sized by its nameplate capacity and coloured by fuel. Plants that burn two fuels, like Jim Bridger, show one circle per fuel.

  • Drag the hour slider, use the arrow keys, or press Play, to step through the day. Each fuel's circles brighten, and its streams thicken, with that fuel's share of PACE generation in that hour.
  • Streams run from every plant into one PACE grid node, then on to the simulated airport. They are a schematic of shares, not real transmission lines: EIA reports fuel totals, not plant-by-plant flows. "Flow off" stops the animation.
  • The dark stream from the node to the airport widens with the airport's simulated draw in that hour (seed 42).
  • Brown circles are coal (dark) and gas (light). Yellow is solar, blue is wind, teal is hydro, grey is everything else.
  • Orange stripes on the slider: PACE's rush hour.
  • Green signs are Wasatch Front cities. Diamonds are the three weather points our load forecast reads. The pin is the simulated airport, at an illustrative location.
