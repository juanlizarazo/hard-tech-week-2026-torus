Six words to know

PACE
The grid operator for Utah, Wyoming and Idaho. All grid data here is its own.
Rush hour
The busiest four hours in PACE's forecast. Orange stripes, every chart.
Demand charge
One busy 15 minutes sets a charge for the whole month.
Marginal CO2
The CO2 from one more MWh in that hour, not the hour's average.
Spin vs Pulse
The flywheel takes short spikes; the battery holds energy for hours.
Capture rate
How close a plan got to the best result possible with hindsight.
