What this is

A linear program is a list of every 15 minutes of the next two days, each with a price for grid energy, carbon and machine wear, and the rules the station must obey: energy balance, charge limits, ramp limits, the Wattsmart reserve.

HiGHS, an open-source solver, finds the cheapest schedule that breaks no rule. The text below is that list, exactly as solved; paste it into any LP solver to check the plan.
